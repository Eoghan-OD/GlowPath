Our Team

GlowPath is a collaborative Agile project developed by students from MTU Cork. The team works under the Scrum framework, dividing the project into sprints, each with clear goals and deliverables. Every member has specific roles beyond development, ensuring all Agile documentation, testing, and deployment requirements are covered.

Eoghan O'Donovan

Lead Developer & Scrum Master

Eoghan manages team coordination, sprint planning, and backlog prioritization. As Lead Developer, he oversees both frontend and backend integration, ensures tasks align with user stories in Jira, and maintains project efficiency. He also supervises version control, repository branching, and merge workflows.

Bradley Neville

Frontend Developer & Documentation Lead

Bradley designs the user interface and handles the visual consistency of the GlowPath website. He is also responsible for preparing documentation, including sprint reports, retrospectives, and presentations, ensuring that all project information is clearly communicated and submission-ready.

Sean Foran

Backend Developer & DevOps Engineer

Sean manages the backend logic of the GlowPath application, handling data storage, import functionality, and process automation. He configures CI/CD pipelines for deployment, maintains the GitHub repository, and ensures consistent integration across all development environments.

Vitalina Sapozhnik

UI/UX Designer & Testing Lead

Vitalina ensures the app is intuitive and accessible through careful UI and UX design. She leads the testing process, validating functionality and interface usability across devices. Her feedback helps improve user experience and ensures the final deliverable meets quality standards.
