Our Team

GlowPath was created by a team of MTU students working together under the Agile Scrum framework. Each sprint has its own goals and tasks, and every member takes on both technical and organisational responsibilities to make sure the project runs smoothly from start to finish.

Eoghan O'Donovan

Lead Developer & Scrum Master

Eoghan organises sprint planning and ensures the team’s progress stays on track. He oversees the app’s technical development, manages GitHub workflows, and coordinates tasks across all members.

Bradley Neville

Frontend Developer & Documentation Lead

Bradley handles the design of the website and keeps the user interface consistent. He also creates sprint reports, retrospectives, and presentation slides so all project details are clearly documented.

Sean Foran

Backend Developer & DevOps Engineer

Sean looks after the logic behind the app, such as handling stored data and automation tasks. He also sets up version control and deployment processes to make sure updates run smoothly.

Vitalina Sapozhnik

UI/UX Designer & Testing Lead

Vitalina focuses on making GlowPath simple and user-friendly. She leads the testing phase, ensuring all pages work properly and that the design is intuitive and accessible for everyone.
