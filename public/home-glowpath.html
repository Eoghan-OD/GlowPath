What Is GlowPath?

GlowPath is a fitness tracking web app focused on being easy to use and simple to understand. It’s built using Agile principles, allowing users to log workouts, view their progress, and gain useful insights without dealing with unnecessary features found in other apps.

Core Features

  • 📝 Easy Data Entry: Log your workouts in seconds.
  • 📂 CSV Uploads: Import your fitness data from a .csv file.
  • 📊 Progress Visualisation: See your performance in charts.
  • 🤖 Smart Insights: Get personalised feedback and trends.
  • 💾 Data Persistence: Workouts are saved automatically to your device.

Our Mission

The GlowPath team aims to show how Agile teamwork can lead to practical, working solutions. Each sprint adds new functionality — starting with data entry, then visualisation, and ending with smart insights.
